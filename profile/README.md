# VisuAll 🎨

> Enhancing digital accessibility through intelligent color adaptation

VisuAll is an accessibility-focused platform that revolutionizes the viewing experience for individuals with color blindness. Our technology dynamically adjusts color palettes in real-time, ensuring that digital content is accessible and enjoyable for everyone, regardless of their color vision capabilities.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To create a more inclusive digital world by providing innovative solutions that empower individuals with color vision deficiencies to experience content in its full vibrancy.

## 🌟 Key Features

- Dynamic color palette adjustment
- Real-time visual enhancement
- Personalized viewing experiences
- Seamless integration capabilities
- User-centric design approach
